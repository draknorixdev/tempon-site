Review instructions (English)

The video shows the actual AccessibilityService detecting the real, logged-out MDU screen: tracking health changes to login preparation and does not classify it as package waiting. Consent is then revoked. Video playback is accelerated to 1.8×; pause to read the complete disclosure. No MDU worker credentials or real customer records are shown.

TEMPON has no account, login, payment, subscription or gated content. All normal screens are available without signing in. On first launch, tap Örnek verilerle dene; later use Ayarlar → Örnek verilerle dene.

The labelled demo runs sample delivery, break and shift-end events through the real calculation and recording engine in an isolated in-memory database. Its History and Analysis screens use fictional records. Exit the demo to inspect normal settings, manual history entry, editing and backup features.

The demo does not read real MDU screens and must not be treated as a live MDU connection. Live automatic tracking observes supported Getir MDU screens only after separate in-app consent and Android Accessibility permission. An independently authorized MDU worker account belongs to the external Getir service, not to TEMPON. TEMPON never asks for, stores or submits MDU credentials.

Veri ve kayıt güvenliği

İzin akışı günlük kurulumdan ayrı önizleme paketinde gösterilir. Örnek demo kayıtları kurgusaldır. TEMPON’un erişim onayı geri çekilebilir; Android takip izni kapatılabilir. Günlük kullanıcının özel kayıtları inceleme dosyalarına eklenmez.
